## 第 5 页　Prior work: four groups of methods

- 4 groups: assignment / route guidance / planners (deadlocks & collisions) / jam handling
- Each fixes one kind of congestion locally

**讲稿**

*【依次指四张卡】* Earlier methods fall into four groups.

> *【依次指四张卡】*前人的方法可以分成四类。

**Order assignment** gives robots close, cheap orders.

> **派单**给车分近的、便宜的单。

**Route guidance** makes busy roads cost more, so robots spread out.

> **选路**让拥挤的路代价更高，把车分散开。

**Planners** decide which robot moves first, and avoid **deadlocks and collisions**.

> **规划**决定哪台车先走，避免**死锁和碰撞**。

**Jam handling** keeps some robots idle, or sorts out robots that meet in a narrow passage.

> **拥堵处理**让一部分车闲着，或者处理在窄道里碰上的车。

*【指底部黄框】* In all the methods we read, each one only fixes **one kind of congestion locally**. None starts from **the whole system** and weighs many factors to **avoid congestion when orders are given out**. [So we wanted to try:]{.underline} is there a **global scheduling algorithm** that avoids congestion?

> *【指底部黄框】*在我们读到的所有方法里，每一种都只是**单独在局部优化某种拥塞**，没有一种从**整个系统**出发、同时考虑多个因素**在派单层面去避免拥塞**，所以我们想进行这个尝试，看看有没有一种**全局调度算法**去避免拥塞。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: a window for each door

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:15　·　配图：窗口大小原理图（从“路上的车数 = 过门速率 × 路程时间”推出；这条计数规律在排队论里叫 Little 定律，讲稿不提名字，只写在图注里；理论示意，附门 6 的数字）**

**提示卡**

- Our measure: robots on the way to a door (hold an order through it, not crossed yet) = window
- Door 6: 13 per 500 ticks each way = 1 per 38.5 ticks
- Trip: 247 ticks free × 2.23 breakdowns ≈ 550 ticks → 550 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3 → 15
- Window = door rate × trip time; fewer: door waits; more: queue, block the other side

**讲稿**

[How many robots should head for door 6 at once?]{.underline} We came up with a way to **measure how many robots are on the way** to a door: robots that hold an order through it but have not crossed yet. This number is our **window**: it decides **how many orders may cross the door at the same time**.

> 同时该有多少车奔向门 6？我们想到一个办法：**衡量在途、会通过某个门口的车有多少**，也就是接了要过这道门的单、还没过门的车。这个数就是我们的**窗口**，用它来决定**同时指派多少个要穿过这道门的任务**。

[How big should the window be?]{.underline} Door 6 lets in **13 robots every 500 ticks** in each direction, **one every 38.5 ticks**. A robot needs **247 ticks** to reach the door on a free path, and random breakdowns make trips **2.23 times** longer: about **550 ticks**. [So]{.underline} to keep the door busy, we send one robot every 38.5 ticks, and **550 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3** robots are on the way at any moment: the window is **15**.

> 窗口该多大？门 6 每个方向每 500 拍能进 **13 台**，也就是**每 38.5 拍一台**。一台车在空路上走到门口要 **247 拍**，随机故障让路程变成 **2.23 倍**，大约 **550 拍**。所以要让门一直有车过，就每 38.5 拍派出一台，路上同时大约有 **550 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3 台**：窗口取 **15**。

[In general,]{.underline} **window = door rate × trip time**. *【指左图上半】* Fewer robots, and the door **waits**; *【指左图下半】* more, and the extras only **queue**, and waiting robots **block the other side**.

> 一般地说，**窗口 = 过门速率 × 路程时间**。*【指左图上半】*车少了，门就**等车**；*【指左图下半】*车多了，多出来的只会**排队**，还会**挡住对面**。

> *【指下方公式】*手调时 8 台太紧，32 台以上又会堵；公式给出**进 15 台、出 10 台**，和手调的最好值一样好。

> *【指左图】*不管门时 **211 台**车奔向门、只过 **3 台**；用名额后大约 25 台，正好在拐点上，能过 **26 台**。

## 第 13 页　Scheduling 2: how long does the door take?

![第 13 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-13.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:40　·　配图：门 6 过门间隔的实测（演讲专用：5 局仿真，官方房间图 250 台，DoorFlow，1255 次过门；figures/deck/demo/door_timing.py）**

**提示卡**

- Measured: 5 runs (seeds 9700–9704), official map, 250 robots, DoorFlow, 1255 crossings of door 6
- Same direction: mostly 6 or 9 ticks = 2 or 3 moves (3 ticks per move); median 10
- Switch: median 34 ticks (door + 4-cell buffer must empty) → +24 per switch

**讲稿**

[How many ticks does the door need per robot? We measured it.]{.underline} We ran **5 simulations**: official map, 250 robots, DoorFlow, 5,000 ticks each, and timed all **1,255 crossings** of door 6.

> 门每过一台车要多少拍？我们直接测：跑了 **5 局仿真**（官方地图、250 台车、DoorFlow，每局 5000 拍），记下门 6 全部 **1255 次过门**的时间。

*【指左图】* In the same direction, the next robot usually comes **6 or 9 ticks** later: that is **2 or 3 moves**, because one move takes **3 ticks** in this competition. The median is **10 ticks**.

> *【指左图】*同一个方向，下一台车通常隔 **6 拍或 9 拍**，也就是 **2 步或 3 步**，因为比赛里走一步要 **3 拍**。中位数是 **10 拍**。

*【指右图】* When the door switches direction, the first robot comes a median of **34 ticks** later, because the door and the 4-cell buffer must empty first. [So]{.underline} every switch costs about **24 extra ticks**.

> *【指右图】*门换方向时，第一台车的中位数要隔 **34 拍**，因为门和 4 格缓冲区要先清空。所以每换一次方向，大约多花 **24 拍**。

## 第 14 页　Scheduling 2: serve one side until it is empty

![第 14 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-14.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:00　·　配图：门速度曲线（演讲专用：U(n) = nh / ((n−1)h + s)，h = 10、s = 34 用上一页的实测中位数）**

**提示卡**

- Door = server with 2 queues, one per direction
- Turn of n: 34 + 10(n − 1) ticks; without switching 10n
- U(n) = 10n ÷ (34 + 10(n − 1)): n = 1 → 29%, n = 10 → 81%; measured 9.3 per turn
- Tests: fixed timetable −5.0%, −6.4%; until empty vs ≤ 80 steps: +1.1% (mostly new room maps)

**讲稿**

The door is like a **server with two queues**, one per direction: which queue should it serve, and for how long?

> 门就像一个**有两个队列的服务台**，每个方向一个队列：先服务哪个队列、服务多久？

*【指公式】* [With the measured numbers,]{.underline} a turn of **n** robots takes **34 + 10 × (n − 1)** ticks, while **10 × n** would be enough without switching. [So]{.underline} the door runs at **U(n) = 10n ÷ (34 + 10(n − 1))** of its top speed. *【指曲线左端】* One robot per turn: **10 ÷ 34 = 29 percent**. *【指 n = 10】* Ten per turn: **100 ÷ 124 = 81 percent**.

> *【指公式】*代入实测的数：一轮放 **n** 台要 **34 + 10 × (n − 1)** 拍，不换向时 **10 × n** 拍就够。所以门的速度是最快速度的 **U(n) = 10n ÷ (34 + 10(n − 1))**。*【指曲线左端】*每轮只放 1 台：**10 ÷ 34 = 29%**。*【指 n = 10】*每轮放 10 台：**100 ÷ 124 = 81%**。

[So]{.underline} we serve one side **until it is empty**; in our runs a turn let through **9.3 robots** on average. [We also tested it:]{.underline} switching on a fixed timetable lost **5.0 and 6.4 percent**, and serving until empty beat a limit of 80 steps per turn by **1.1 percent**, mostly on new room maps.

> 所以让一边**走空再换**；在我们的仿真里，平均每轮放 **9.3 台**。我们也做了实验：按固定时间表换向少了 **5.0% 和 6.4%**；走空再换比每轮最多放 80 步多 **1.1%**，主要来自新生成的房间图。

## 第 15 页　Scheduling 2: how big should the buffer be?

![第 15 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-15.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:05　·　配图：门口规则详图 B、C：缓冲区大小扫描（12 个开发实例）；门 6 每过一台 18.7 拍的分解（第 52 轮 669 次过门）；下限 ⌈9 ÷ 3⌉ = 3 格的公式见 netmac.cpp 第 59–62 行注释（第 57 轮）**

**提示卡**

- Exit: a robot every 9–10 ticks, 3 ticks per move → ~3 cells → wait at least 3 cells back
- Sweep (12 dev, vs 4): 1 cell −9.0%, 2 −5.2%, 3 even (confirm −2.64%, deadlocks 11 → 29), 6 −4.2% → 4 = minimum + 1
- 18.7 ticks per crossing (round 52); breakdowns 4.8; 500 ÷ 18.7 ≈ 26.7 ≈ 26
- Hand over to Speaker 4

**讲稿**

*【指上图 B】* [How big should the buffer be?]{.underline} Robots come out of the door every **9 to 10 ticks**, and one move takes **3 ticks**, so a robot that just came out is only about **3 cells** away when the next one comes out. [So]{.underline} the waiting side must stand **at least 3 cells** back, or robots coming out get stuck.

> *【指上图 B】*缓冲区该多大？门里每 **9 到 10 拍**出来一台车，走一步要 **3 拍**，所以下一台出来时，前一台只走出大约 **3 格**。所以等着的一边至少要退后 **3 格**，不然出来的车会被堵住。

[Then we tested]{.underline} 1 to 6 cells on **12 development instances**: 1 and 2 cells lost **9.0 and 5.2 percent**; 3 cells looked even, but a confirmation test lost **2.64 percent**, with deadlocked runs up from **11 to 29**; 6 cells lost **4.2 percent**, as the queue blocked other doors. [So we use]{.underline} **4 cells**: the minimum plus one.

> 然后我们实测了 1 到 6 格（**12 个开发实例**）：1 格、2 格分别少 **9.0%、5.2%**；3 格看起来持平，但确认测试少 **2.64%**，堵死的局从 **11 局增到 29 局**；6 格少 **4.2%**，因为排队的车挡住了别的门。所以用 **4 格**：下限再多留 1 格。

*【指下图 C】* The buffer also keeps **random pauses** out of the exit: each robot needs **18.7 ticks** at door 6, **4.8** of them breakdowns; and **500 ÷ 18.7 ≈ 26.7** is where the door's **26** comes from. [Over to Speaker 4.]{.underline}

> *【指下图 C】*缓冲区也让**随机暂停**不会堵在出口：门 6 每过一台要 **18.7 拍**，其中 **4.8 拍**是故障；**500 ÷ 18.7 ≈ 26.7**，这就是门每 500 拍过 **26 台**的来历。下面交给讲者 4。
