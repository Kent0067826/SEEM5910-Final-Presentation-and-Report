## 第 7 页　The model: 16 rooms, 31 links, routers at the doors

**提示卡**

- Sender / packet / half-duplex link / router port
- Router at the door: 4.2×; 30% of waiting on 10% of area

**讲稿**

Thank you. To borrow network methods, we first needed a model. *【指左图】* We turned the 3,646 walkable cells into a network: **16 rooms become 16 nodes, and 31 doors become 31 links**.

> 谢谢。要借网络的方法，先要有模型。*【指左图】*我们把 3646 个可走格子压成一张网络：**16 个房间变成 16 个点，31 道门变成 31 条线**。

> *【指红线门 6】*有一条线很特别：**门 6 是一个割**。拿掉它，左下 4 个房间就和其他房间断开了。

*【指右边四行】* The **order center is the sender**. A **robot is a packet**. A **door is a half-duplex link**, a link that carries one direction at a time. The area **within eight steps of a door is the router port**.

> *【指右边四行】*逐项对应：**派单中心是发送端**，**机器人是数据包**，**门是半双工链路**，也就是一次只走一个方向的线路；**门口 8 步以内是路由器端口**。

*【指黄色框】* Why at the door? We measured it. One to three steps from a door, robots spend **4.2 times** as long standing still as moving. The door cell and two steps on each side are **only 10 percent of the area, but hold 30 percent of all waiting**. Time is lost at the door.

> *【指黄色框】*为什么放在门口？我们实测过：离门 1 到 3 步，车停着的时间是走的 **4.2 倍**；门格和两侧各 2 步**只占 10% 的面积，却占了 30% 的等待**。时间就丢在门口。

## 第 8 页　Similar, but not the same

![第 8 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-08.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:50　·　配图：图 5 仓库与网络的对应**

**提示卡**

- 1 cannot be dropped
- 2 waiting robot blocks a cell
- 3 fixed fleet
- 4 no retransmission (1986 vs face to face)
- Closer to a lossless data-center network

**讲稿**

But similar is not the same. *【指右边四张卡】* There are four differences.

> 但像不等于一样。*【指右边四张卡】*有四点不同。

**One: robots cannot be dropped.** A router can throw a packet away. A robot can only wait. **Two: a waiting robot takes up a cell**, and it may block a robot coming the other way.

> **第一，机器人不能丢。** 路由器可以把包扔掉，车只能等。**第二，等着的车占着格子**，可能挡住对面来的车。

**Three: the number of robots is fixed.** Robots stay on the map after they deliver. **Four: the cause of the jam is different.** The 1986 internet collapse came from **sending the same data again and again**. Here, robots just meet face to face.

> **第三，车数固定。** 送完货车还在地图上。**第四，堵的原因不同。** 1986 年互联网崩溃来自**同样的数据一遍遍重发**；这里是车面对面顶住。

*【指底部一行】* So we do not borrow methods made for resending. Robots are closer to a **lossless data-center network**, one that never drops data.

> *【指底部一行】*所以专门对付重发的办法我们不借。机器人更接近数据中心的**无损网络**，也就是从不丢数据的网络。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: a per-door quota, like a TCP window

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:20　·　配图：图 9 在途车与过门数；图 8 名额公式**

**提示卡**

- TCP window: sent but not acknowledged
- We count: holds a door-6 order, not crossed, even far away
- Full → assign an order that does not cross
- 2.23 × 247 ÷ 500 × 13 → 15 in, 10 out
- Nothing dropped, fixed → must test
- Red: 211 in flight, 3 cross; blue: 25 in flight, 26 cross

**讲稿**

Our first scheduling method is a **quota for each door**. It comes from the **TCP window**. In TCP, the sender limits data that is **sent but not yet confirmed**.

> 第一个调度方法是**每门名额**。它借自 **TCP 窗口**：TCP 里，发送端限制**已经发出、还没确认**的数据。

We limit robots that **already have an order through door 6 but have not crossed yet**, even if they are far away. *【指右边三条】* When the quota is full, the center gives that robot **an order that does not use the door**.

> 我们限制**已经接了要过门 6 的单、还没过门**的车，哪怕它离门还很远。*【指右边三条】*名额满了，派单中心就给这台车派**一单不过门的**。

*【指黄色框】* The quota size is **bandwidth times delay**: a slowdown of **2.23** from breakdowns, times **247 ticks** to reach the door, divided by 500, times **13 robots per 500 ticks** in each direction. That gives **15 going in and 10 going out**.

> *【指黄色框】*名额大小按**带宽乘时延**算：故障放慢 **2.23** × 走到门口 **247 拍** ÷ 500 × 每个方向每 500 拍 **13 台**，得到**进 15 台、出 10 台**。

Unlike TCP, **nothing is dropped and the quota is fixed**, so we had to test it.

> 和 TCP 不同，**这里不丢包、名额固定**，所以有没有用必须实验检验。

*【指左图红线】* In red, with no control, robots on the way to the door climb to **211**, and only **3** get through every 500 ticks. *【指蓝点】* In blue, with the quota, about **25** are on the way, and the door lets through **26 on average**. Too many robots do not go faster. **They get stuck.**

> *【指左图红线】*红线不管门，在途车涨到 **211 台**，每 500 拍只过 **3 台**。*【指蓝点】*蓝点用名额，在途 25 台左右，门平均过 **26 台**。车放多了不会过得更快，**只会堵死**。

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 2: one side at a time, switch when empty

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:15　·　配图：图 10 门口规则示意；图 11 门利用率公式**

**提示卡**

- Half-duplex + polling
- Same direction 9 ticks; switch 21 ticks
- Switch every robot: 9/21 = 43%
- Batches of 10: 90/102 = 88%
- Serve one side → drain → switch, no timer
- 4-cell zone: no network counterpart

**讲稿**

The second method is the **door rule**. It comes from **half-duplex links and polling**, which means serving each side in turn. A one-cell door is a half-duplex link: **one direction at a time**.

> 第二个方法是**门口规则**，借自**半双工链路和轮询**，也就是两边轮流服务。一格宽的门就是半双工链路：**一次一个方向**。

We measured two costs. In the same direction, a robot follows every **9 ticks**. But changing direction takes **21 ticks**, because the door and the other side must clear first.

> 我们测了两个代价：同方向每 **9 拍**跟过一台；换方向要 **21 拍**，因为门格和对面要先清空。

*【指 43%】* If the door changes direction after every robot, it is busy only **9 out of 21, or 43 percent** of the time. *【指 88%】* With groups of ten, it is **90 out of 102, or 88 percent**.

> *【指 43%】*每过一台就换向，门只有 **9 ÷ 21 = 43%** 的时间在过车。*【指 88%】*一批放 10 台，是 **90 ÷ 102 = 88%**。

*【指上面三格图，从左到右】* So the door serves one side. When that side is empty, it finishes, and then it changes direction. **No timer is needed.**

> *【指上面三格图，从左到右】*所以门先服务一边，这一边走空了就收尾，然后换向，**不需要定时器**。

*【指 4 cells】* One rule has **nothing like it in networks**: robots on the other side **step back out of a four-cell zone**, so the exit stays clear. A waiting packet never blocks a cable, but **a waiting robot blocks the way**.

> *【指 4 cells】*还有一条规则**网络里没有**：对面的车**退出 4 格的门口区**，把出口让出来。等着的数据包不会挡住网线，但**等着的车会挡路**。

- Credit flow control: return a freed slot at once
- Executor kept robot in old cell's queue
- 3 lines → 26
- Global cap 138 (Little's law)
- Watchdog 300 ticks; relax rule, never drop
- Hand over to Speaker 4

**讲稿**

Third, a **bug fix**. Even with both rules, door 6 was **stuck at 22** robots every 500 ticks. In **credit flow control**, when a slot is free, the sender must be told **at once**.

> 第三是**修一个程序错误**。两条规则都上了，门 6 还是**卡在每 500 拍 22 台**。**信用流控**里，空位一腾出来就要**立刻**告诉发送端。

*【指上排】* We checked tick by tick. The official executor **kept a robot in a cell's waiting list after it had left the cell**. So the next robot waited a whole planning round for nothing.

> *【指上排】*一拍一拍查下来，发现官方执行器**在车离开一格后，那一格的排队名单里还留着它**，后面的车白等一整个规划周期。

*【指下排和 22 → 26】* We changed **three lines**, and the door went back to **26**.

> *【指下排和 22 → 26】*改了 **3 行**，门回到 **26 台**。

*【指右上】* Last, **two safety nets**. The first is a **global cap** from Little's law: 1.76 times 69.2 divided by 500, times 2.23, times 255, gives **138 robots** with orders at most.

> *【指右上】*最后是**两个兜底**。第一个是按 Little 定律定的**全图上限**：1.76 × 69.2 ÷ 500 × 2.23 × 255，得到同时接单最多 **138 台**。

*【指右下】* The second is a **deadlock watchdog**, from lossless networks. If a robot has waited **300 ticks**, and no robot has entered its door for 300 ticks, it may skip the door rule for one tick. Switches drop packets to break a deadlock. **We cannot drop robots, so we relax the rule instead.**

> *【指右下】*第二个是借自无损网络的**死锁看门狗**：车等满 **300 拍**、它所在的门 300 拍没有车进，就允许它这一拍不守门口规则。交换机靠丢包走出死锁，**我们不能丢车，所以改成放宽规则**。
