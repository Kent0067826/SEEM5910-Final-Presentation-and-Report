*【指底部黄框】* In all the methods we read, each one only fixes **one kind of congestion locally**. None starts from **the whole system** and weighs many factors to **avoid congestion when orders are given out**. So we wanted to try: is there a **global scheduling algorithm** that avoids congestion?

> *【指底部黄框】*在我们读到的所有方法里，每一种都只是**单独在局部优化某种拥塞**，没有一种从**整个系统**出发、同时考虑多个因素**在派单层面去避免拥塞**，所以我们想进行这个尝试，看看有没有一种**全局调度算法**去避免拥塞。

So why did changing routes not help? A simple calculation explains it.

> 那为什么改路线没有用？一个简单的计算就能说明。

> *【指 1】*左下房间占 3614 个房间格里的 **906 个**，也就是 906 ÷ 3614 = **25%**。

*【指 2】* An order needs door 6 when its two ends are on different sides: **2 times 0.25 times 0.75, or 37.6 percent** of orders; the task file gives 38.5.

> *【指 2】*一单两头落在门的两侧，就要过门 6：**2 × 0.25 × 0.75 = 37.6%**；任务文件里是 38.5%。

*【指 3 和公式】* Door 6 passes **26 robots** every 500 ticks, so the map can finish at most **26 divided by 0.376, about 69 orders**, whatever routes robots take.

> *【指 3 和公式】*门 6 每 500 拍过 **26 台**，所以不管车怎么走，整张图最多完成 **26 ÷ 0.376 ≈ 69 单**。

*【指底部】* So the real question is scheduling: **how many robots to send, and which side goes first**. This is the idea of our algorithm, **DoorFlow**: regulate the flow through every door, for the whole map.

> *【指底部】*所以真正的问题是调度：**派多少车、哪边先走**。这就是我们的算法 **DoorFlow** 的想法：从全图出发，调控每道门的流量。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: the logic of TCP's window

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:00　·　配图：在途量与带宽时延积原理图（理论示意）**

**提示卡**

- TCP window = data on the way; Little's law: on the way = rate × trip time
- Door max 26 / 500 ticks
- Too few: door waits; too many: rate stuck at 26 → extra only queues
- Best: rate × trip time with no queue = bandwidth-delay product = TCP's target window
- Robots: past it, waiting robots block the other side → rate falls
- Quota = bandwidth × delay

**讲稿**

How many robots should head for door 6 at once? TCP faces the same question with its **window**, the amount of data on the way. The logic is **Little's law**: what is on the way equals **rate times trip time**.

> 同时该有多少车奔向门 6？TCP 的**窗口**面对的是同一个问题：窗口就是路上的数据量。背后的逻辑是 **Little 定律**：路上的量 = **速率 × 路程时间**。

*【指左图上半】* The door passes at most **26 robots** every 500 ticks. With too few on the way, the door **waits**, and capacity is wasted. With too many, the rate cannot rise above 26, so the extra robots only make the trip longer: *【指左图下半】* they **queue**.

> *【指左图上半】*门每 500 拍最多过 **26 台**。路上的车太少，门就**等车**，能力浪费；太多，速率也超不过 26，多出来的车只会把路程拉长：*【指左图下半】*它们在**排队**。

*【指橙色虚线】* So the best number is **rate times the trip time with no queue**: the **bandwidth-delay product**, which is the window TCP aims for.

> *【指橙色虚线】*所以最好的车数是**速率 × 不排队时的路程时间**，也就是**带宽时延积**，正是 TCP 窗口要瞄准的大小。

*【指右边最后一条】* For robots, going past it is worse than a queue: waiting robots **block the other side**, so the rate **falls**. That is why our quota is **bandwidth times delay**.

> *【指右边最后一条】*对车来说，超过这个点比排队还糟：等着的车会**挡住对面**，速率反而**下降**。所以我们的名额定为**带宽 × 时延**。

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 1: global regulation, like BBR

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:15　·　配图：BBR 控制环与 DoorFlow 对照图（含第 50 轮实测时延比）**

**提示卡**

- Goal: global regulation; a buffer size (quota) per door & direction
- Reno / CUBIC: grow until loss; robots never lost → queue grows
- BBR: max delivery rate × min RTT; min keeps a jam from raising its own limit
- Our delay: real time (Little) vs shortest-path time; missed spots 2.2–2.9×
- BBR-style cap: big losses gone, small gain +0.08% → fixed quota; watchdog later

**讲稿**

Our goal is **global regulation**: controlling the crossings at every door at once, with a **buffer size**, the quota, for each door and direction. How should we set these limits?

> 我们的目标是**全局调控**：同时控制每一道门的过门车数，每道门、每个方向有一个**缓冲区大小**，也就是名额。这些上限该怎么定？

Ordinary TCP, like **Reno or CUBIC**, grows its window **until a packet is lost**. Robots are never lost, so the queue just grows.

> 普通 TCP，比如 **Reno、CUBIC**，会一直开大窗口，**直到丢包**。车永远不会丢，所以队只会越排越长。

*【指左图第 2 框】* **BBR** measures instead. Bandwidth is the **highest delivery rate** seen, since delivery is never faster than the true limit; delay is the **shortest round-trip time** seen, since a queue only adds delay. *【指右上橙框】* If the limit followed the current delay, a jam would raise its own limit.

> *【指左图第 2 框】***BBR** 则是去测：带宽取看到过的**最大送达速率**，因为实际送达不会比真实上限快；时延取看到过的**最短往返时间**，因为排队只会增加时延。*【指右上橙框】*如果上限跟着当前时延走，堵车就会把自己的上限抬高。

*【指下方对照表】* We estimate delay the same way: the **shortest-path time** is the delay with no queue, and the **real time**, from Little's law, includes waiting. *【指右下柱状图】* On spots our first model missed, the real time was **2.2 to 2.9 times** the model: robots were stuck there.

> *【指下方对照表】*我们用同样的办法估计时延：**最短路时间**是不排队时的时延，用 Little 定律算出的**真实时间**包含了等待。*【指右下柱状图】*在第一版模型漏掉的窄处，真实时间是模型的 **2.2 到 2.9 倍**：那里有车卡住了。

A **BBR-style cap** on those spots removed the big losses, but the overall gain was small, **0.08 percent**, so we kept the fixed quota; stuck robots are handled by a watchdog.

> 在这些窄处试的 **BBR 式上限**消除了大的损失，但总体提升不大，只有 **0.08%**，所以保留固定名额；卡住的车交给看门狗处理（后面讲）。

## 第 12 页　Scheduling 1: the quota, version by version

![第 12 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-12.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:55　·　配图：图 9 在途车与过门数；图 8 名额公式**

**提示卡**

- Count: holds a door-6 order, not crossed, even far away; full → order that does not cross
- v1 (round 25): one window for all: +0.53% on the champion (small)
- v2 (rounds 28–29): per door & direction → +29.1% alone (17 room-map instances)
- Size: 8 too tight, 32+ jams → formula 15 in / 10 out (round 41), no worse than hand-tuned (+0.63%)
- No control 211 → 3; quota ~25 (knee) → 26

**讲稿**

The quota counts every robot that **holds an order through door 6 but has not crossed yet**; when it is full, the next robot gets **an order that does not need the door**.

> 名额数的是所有**接了过门 6 的单、还没过门**的车；名额满了，下一台车就派**一单不用过门的**。

*【指右边第二条】* Our first window counted all robots together and gained only **0.53 percent**. *【指右边第三条】* Counting **per door and per direction** gained **29.1 percent** on its own, on 17 room-map instances.

> *【指右边第二条】*第一版窗口把所有车一起数，只多了 **0.53%**。*【指右边第三条】*改成**按门、按方向**数，单独就多了 **29.1%**（17 个房间图实例）。

*【指公式】* Hand-tuned sizes were too tight at 8 and jammed at 32 or more, so we derived the size: **2.23 times 247 divided by 500 times 13 is 14.3**, giving **15 in** and **10 out**, as good as the best hand-tuned value.

> *【指公式】*手调时 8 台太紧，32 台以上又会堵，所以我们用公式推出大小：**2.23 × 247 ÷ 500 × 13 = 14.3**，得到**进 15 台、出 10 台**，和手调的最好值一样好。

## 第 14 页　Scheduling 2: queue scheduling at the door

![第 14 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-14.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:45　·　配图：图 11 门利用率公式（演讲专用版：公式和数字相同，只把标题改成不带网络术语）**

**提示卡**

- Door = server with 2 queues, one per direction
- Switch 21 ticks; same direction 9 ticks
- Every robot: 43%; batch of 10: 88%
- v1: at most 80 steps → extra switches → exhaustive service
- Theory assumes independent arrivals → tested

**讲稿**

Looking closer, the door is a **server with two queues**, one per direction. Which queue should it serve, and for how long?

> 再往细看，门是一个**有两个队列的服务台**，每个方向一个队列。先服务哪个队列、服务多久？

*【指公式】* Switching costs **21 ticks**, while robots in the same direction follow every **9**. *【指 43%】* Switching after every robot keeps the door busy only **43 percent** of the time; *【指 88%】* ten per turn gives **88 percent**.

> *【指公式】*换方向要 **21 拍**，同方向每 **9 拍**就能跟过一台。*【指 43%】*每过一台就换，门只有 **43%** 的时间在过车；*【指 88%】*每次放 10 台是 **88%**。

*【指右边第三条】* Our first version capped each turn at **80 steps**, which caused extra switches, so we moved to **exhaustive service**: serve one queue **until it is empty**.

> *【指右边第三条】*第一版每轮最多放 **80 步**，结果多换了不少次方向，所以改成**穷尽服务**：一个队列**服务到走空**再换。

*【指底部】* Theory calls this best when switching is costly, but assumes arrivals do not depend on the scheduler, so we tested it.

> *【指底部】*理论上换向代价大时这样最好，但前提是车什么时候到和调度无关，所以我们用实验检验。

## 第 21 页　Lessons: why DoorFlow looks like this

![第 21 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-21.png)

**讲者 4　·　0:50　·　配图：五条经验卡片（幻灯片上；数字取自报告第九节）**

**提示卡**

- Lessons from all versions on slide 6
- Door 6 cannot be bypassed: 14 of 15 rerouting lower → control how many, not where
- Cannot drop, take cells → only decide who goes and when
- Waiting less ≠ crossing more: credit flow −2.74% → judge by orders finished
- Few maps only (7 of 69) → confirm on new maps; measure first (38 of 69 stopped before code)

**讲稿**

Looking back at all the versions on slide 6, five lessons explain **why DoorFlow ended up this way**.

> 回头看第 6 页的所有版本，有五条经验解释了 **DoorFlow 为什么是现在这个样子**。

*【指第一张卡】* Door 6 cannot be bypassed: **14 of 15** rerouting methods lowered results, so we control **how many robots go**, not where. *【指第二张卡】* Robots cannot be dropped and take up cells, so methods built on dropping or buffering do not carry over; we only decide **who goes and when**.

> *【指第一张卡】*门 6 绕不开：15 种换路办法里 **14 种**都变差，所以我们管**派多少车**，不管走哪条路。*【指第二张卡】*车不能丢、还占着格子，所以靠丢包或缓冲的办法用不上；我们只决定**谁走、什么时候走**。

*【指第三张卡】* Waiting less is not crossing more: **hop-by-hop credit flow** cut waiting but lowered results by **2.74 percent**, so we judge every change by orders finished. *【指第四、五张卡】* Gains on a few maps do not count, so we confirm on new maps; and we **measure the possible gain first**: 38 of 69 ideas stopped there, before any code.

> *【指第三张卡】*等得少不等于过得多：**逐跳信用流控**让车等得少了，结果却少了 **2.74%**，所以每项改动都按完成的单数来判断。*【指第四、五张卡】*只在少数图上有提升不算数，所以要换新图确认；而且**先量能省多少**：69 种里有 38 种量完就停了，没写代码。
