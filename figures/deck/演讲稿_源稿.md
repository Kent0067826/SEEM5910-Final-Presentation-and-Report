## 第 7 页　The model, and how robots differ from packets

![第 7 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-07.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:00　·　配图：图 4 地图压成网络**

**提示卡**

- 16 rooms = nodes, 31 doors = links; door 6 is a cut
- Sender / packet / half-duplex link / router at the door (4.2×)
- Differences: cannot drop; waiting blocks a cell; fixed fleet; face to face, not resending (1986)
- So: skip resend methods; closer to a lossless network

**讲稿**

Thank you. Let me start with the model. *【指左图】* The **16 rooms become nodes**, and the **31 doors become links**. *【指红线门 6】* **Door 6 is a cut**: remove it, and the four lower-left rooms are cut off.

> 谢谢。先讲模型。*【指左图】***16 个房间变成点，31 道门变成线**。*【指红线门 6】***门 6 是一个割**：拿掉它，左下 4 个房间就断开了。

*【指右上四行】* The part that gives out orders is the **sender**, each **robot is a packet**, and each **door is a half-duplex link**. The area near a door is the **router**, because robots there stand still **4.2 times** as long as they move.

> *【指右上四行】*负责派单的部分是**发送端**，每台**车是一个数据包**，每道**门是一条半双工链路**。门口附近是**路由器**，因为车在那里停着的时间是走的 **4.2 倍**。

*【指右下四点】* But robots are not packets: they **cannot be dropped**, a waiting robot **takes up a cell**, the fleet is **fixed**, and jams come from robots meeting **face to face**, not from resending data as in the 1986 internet collapse.

> *【指右下四点】*但车不是数据包：车**不能丢**；等着的车**占着格子**；车数**固定**；而且堵车是因为车**面对面顶住**，不是像 1986 年互联网崩溃那样因为数据重发。

*【指底部】* So we skip methods built for resending. Robots are closer to a **lossless network**, one that never drops data.

> *【指底部】*所以专门对付重发的办法我们不借。车更接近**无损网络**，也就是从不丢数据的网络。

> *【指底部 11】*我们测的 **11 种换路办法确实全部变差**。所以真正的问题是**派多少车、哪边先走**，这是调度。

## 第 9 页　Scheduling 1: the TCP window principle

![第 9 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-09.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:40　·　配图：TCP / BBR 概念图（示意，不是实测数据）**

**提示卡**

- How many robots should head for door 6 at once?
- TCP window: sent but not yet confirmed
- Too little in flight: link idle; too much: only a queue
- Best: in flight = bandwidth × delay; BBR aims here
- Robots are worse: extra robots block the other side → crossings fall
- So cap robots heading for the door at bandwidth × delay

**讲稿**

Our first method decides how many robots head for door 6 at once. In networks, we use the **TCP window** to limit data that is **sent but not yet confirmed**.

> 第一个方法决定：同时该有多少车奔向门 6。在网络里，我们用 **TCP 窗口**来管这件事：它限制**已经发出、还没确认**的数据有多少。

*【指左图】* If too little data is on the way, the link **sits idle**; if too much, the extra data **just waits in a queue**. *【指橙色虚线】* The best amount is **bandwidth times delay**.

> *【指左图】*路上的数据太少，链路会**空着**；太多，多出来的数据**只会排队**。*【指橙色虚线】*最好的量是**带宽 × 时延**。

*【指右边最后两条】* For robots, too many is worse: extra robots **block the other side**, and crossings **fall**. That is why we cap the robots heading for the door at **bandwidth times delay**.

> *【指右边最后两条】*对车来说，太多更糟：多出来的车会**挡住对面**，过门数反而**下降**。所以我们把奔向门的车限制在**带宽 × 时延**。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: why BBR, not ordinary TCP

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:45　·　配图：BBR 与丢包型 TCP 概念图（示意，不是实测数据）**

**提示卡**

- Reno / CUBIC: grow until a packet is lost
- Robots never dropped → no stop signal → 211 in flight
- BBR: max delivery rate × min round-trip time, no loss needed
- Use the MIN delay: a jam makes delay longer → would raise the window
- Our quota: measured bandwidth × travel time
- Rounds 50–51: BBR-style cap on missed narrow spots (delay 2.2–2.9× model): big losses gone, +0.08% → keep fixed quota
- Not copied: BBR probing state machine, pacing

**讲稿**

But not every kind of TCP fits robots. Ordinary TCP, like **Reno or CUBIC**, keeps growing its window **until a packet is lost**. *【指左图红线】* Robots are **never lost**, so such a window would never stop growing; in fact, we saw **211** robots heading for one door.

> 但不是每种 TCP 都适合机器人。普通 TCP，比如 **Reno、CUBIC**，会一直把窗口开大，**直到丢包**。*【指左图红线】*而车**永远不会丢**，所以这种窗口会一直涨下去；我们实际看到的正是 **211 台**车奔向同一道门。

*【指左图蓝线】* **BBR** does not wait for a loss. It measures the **highest delivery rate** and the **shortest round-trip time**, and keeps the data in flight near their product. It uses the **shortest** time, because a jam makes delay longer.

> *【指左图蓝线】***BBR** 不等丢包：它测出**最大送达速率**和**最短往返时间**，让路上的数据保持在两者乘积附近。它用**最短**时间，是因为堵车会把时延拉长。

*【指右边下框】* Our quota follows this idea. We also tried a **BBR-style cap** on spots our model missed, but it gained only **0.08 percent**.

> *【指右边下框】*我们的名额用的就是这个思路。我们也在模型漏掉的窄处试过 **BBR 式上限**，但只多了 **0.08%**。

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 1: our per-door quota = bandwidth × delay

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:05　·　配图：图 9 在途车与过门数；图 8 名额公式**

**提示卡**

- Count: holds a door-6 order, not crossed, even far away
- Full → give an order that does not cross
- Bandwidth 26 / 500 ticks → 13 per direction
- Delay 247 ticks × 2.23 → 15 in; 158 ticks → 10 out
- Red: 211 in flight, 3 cross; blue: about 25 (knee), 26 cross
- AIMD (self-adjusting) worse than fixed → keep formula

**讲稿**

Our quota counts every robot that **has an order through door 6 but has not crossed yet**, even if it is far away. *【指右边第一条】* When the quota is full, the next robot gets **an order that does not need the door**.

> 我们的名额数的是所有**接了过门 6 的单、还没过门**的车，哪怕它离门还很远。*【指右边第一条】*名额满了，下一台车就派**一单不用过门的**。

*【指公式】* Both numbers in the formula, bandwidth and delay, are measured. Door 6 passes **26 robots** every 500 ticks, or **13 per direction**. A robot needs **247 ticks** to reach the door, and breakdowns slow it by **2.23 times**. So **2.23 times 247 divided by 500 times 13 is 14.3**, which rounds up to **15 going in**. Going out takes 158 ticks, which gives **10**.

> *【指公式】*公式里的两个数，带宽和时延，都是实测的。门 6 每 500 拍过 **26 台**，也就是每个方向 **13 台**。车走到门口要 **247 拍**，故障让它慢 **2.23 倍**。所以 **2.23 × 247 ÷ 500 × 13 = 14.3**，向上取整为**进的方向 15 台**；出来要走 158 拍，算出 **10 台**。

*【指左图红线】* In the test, with no control, **211** robots head for the door and only **3** get through. *【指蓝点】* With the quota, about **25** head for it, right at the knee, and **26** get through.

> *【指左图红线】*实验里，不管门时有 **211 台**车奔向门，只过 **3 台**。*【指蓝点】*用名额后，大约 25 台奔向门，正好在拐点上，能过 **26 台**。

*【指右边最后一条】* Unlike TCP, nothing is dropped and the quota is **fixed**; a self-adjusting window, **AIMD**, did worse.

> *【指右边最后一条】*和 TCP 不同，这里不丢包，名额也是**固定**的；会自己调整的窗口 **AIMD** 反而更差。

## 第 12 页　Scheduling 2: one direction at a time

![第 12 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-12.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:30　·　配图：图 10 门口规则示意**

**提示卡**

- 1-cell door = half-duplex link: one direction at a time
- Serve one side; other side steps back 4 cells
- Side empty → wind down → switch
- 4-cell step-back: no network counterpart

**讲稿**

Our second method is the **door rule**. A one-cell door works like a **half-duplex link**: it carries **one direction at a time**.

> 第二个方法是**门口规则**。一格宽的门就像一条**半双工链路**：**一次只走一个方向**。

*【指上面三格图，从左到右】* So the door serves one side, and robots on the other side **step back four cells** to keep the exit clear. When that side is empty, the door **switches direction**.

> *【指上面三格图，从左到右】*所以门先服务一边，对面的车**退出门口 4 格**，把出口让出来。这一边走空了，门再**换方向**。

*【指 4 cells】* This step-back rule has **no match in networks**: a waiting packet sits in a buffer, but **a waiting robot blocks the way**.

> *【指 4 cells】*退让这一条在**网络里没有**：等着的数据包待在缓存里，但**等着的车会挡路**。

## 第 13 页　Scheduling 2: queue scheduling at the door

![第 13 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-13.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:10　·　配图：图 11 门利用率公式**

**提示卡**

- Door = router port with 2 queues (one per direction, robots within 8 cells)
- Which queue, for how long = queue scheduling
- 9 ticks same direction, 21 ticks to switch
- Switch every robot 43%; batch of 10: 88%
- Tried: limit 80 steps → kept: exhaustive service (serve until empty)
- Blocked robots → lowest planner priority
- Polling theory assumes arrivals independent: not true here → tested

**讲稿**

Looking closer, the door is like a **router port with two queues**, one per direction. Which queue should it serve, and for how long? That is **queue scheduling**.

> 再往细看，门就像一个**有两个队列的路由器端口**，每个方向一个队列。先服务哪个队列、服务多久？这就是**队列调度**。

*【指公式】* Switching is costly: a robot can follow in the same direction every **9 ticks**, but changing direction takes **21**. *【指 43%】* If the door switches after every robot, like **round-robin**, it is busy only **9 out of 21 ticks, or 43 percent**. *【指 88%】* If it lets ten through each time, that rises to **88 percent**.

> *【指公式】*换方向代价很大：同方向每 **9 拍**就能跟过一台，换方向却要 **21 拍**。*【指 43%】*如果每过一台就换，像**轮转**那样，门只有 **9 ÷ 21 = 43%** 的时间在过车。*【指 88%】*每次放 10 台，就升到 **90 ÷ 102 = 88%**。

*【指右边第三条】* So we use **polling**. Our first version capped each turn at 80 steps; we then switched to **exhaustive service**, where the door serves one queue **until it is empty**. Polling theory says this is best when switching is costly.

> *【指右边第三条】*所以我们用**轮询**。最早的版本每轮最多放 80 步，后来改成**穷尽服务**：一个队列**服务到走空**再换。轮询理论说，换向代价大时这样最好。

*【指右边第四条】* Robots stopped by the rule get the **lowest priority** in the planner, so they do not hold up others.

> *【指右边第四条】*被规则拦住的车在规划里排到**最低优先级**，不会耽误别的车。

*【指底部】* But polling theory assumes arrivals do not depend on the scheduler; here they depend on our quota, so we tested it.

> *【指底部】*不过，轮询理论假设车什么时候到和调度规则无关；这里车什么时候到取决于我们的名额，所以我们用实验检验了它。

> *【指左下卡片】*第一，有些图上有**门口规则管不到的窄处**。所以我们按 Little 定律加了一个**全图上限**，限制同时接单的车数；在官方图上算出来是 **138 台**（算式在卡片上）。*【指左图第二栏】*拿掉这个上限，一张新图在 249 台时少了 **16.9%**。

> *【指右下卡片】*第二，**死锁**虽然少见，但会发生：有一局里两道只隔一步的门互相锁住，完成数从 **1114 单掉到 700 单**。所以我们借了无损网络的**看门狗**：车等满 **300 拍**、它的门也 300 拍没有车进，就允许它这一拍不守门口规则。网络在这时会丢包；**我们改成放宽规则**。

*【指左图第三栏】* Deadlocks are rare, so on these 8 maps the watchdog shows **no measurable effect**. But in an earlier test on **15 generated room-map instances**, it added **2.30 percent**, so we keep both safety nets.

> *【指左图第三栏】*死锁很少见，所以看门狗在这 8 张图上**测不出作用**；但在更早一次、**15 个生成房间图实例**上的测试里，它多了 **2.30%**。所以两个兜底都留着。
