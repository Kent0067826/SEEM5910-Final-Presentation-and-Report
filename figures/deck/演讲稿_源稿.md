## 第 8 页　Similar, but not the same

![第 8 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-08.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:50　·　配图：图 5 仓库与网络的对应**

**提示卡**

- 1 cannot be dropped
- 2 waiting robot blocks a cell
- 3 fixed fleet
- 4 no retransmission (1986 vs face to face)
- Closer to a lossless data-center network

**讲稿**

But similar is not the same. *【指右边四张卡】* There are four differences.

> 但像不等于一样。*【指右边四张卡】*有四点不同。

**One: robots cannot be dropped**; a router can drop a packet, a robot can only wait. **Two: a waiting robot blocks a cell.** **Three: the number of robots is fixed.**

> **第一，车不能丢**：路由器可以丢包，车只能等。**第二，等着的车占着格子。** **第三，车数固定。**

**Four: the cause is different.** The 1986 internet collapse came from **resending data**. Robots do not resend; they just meet face to face.

> **第四，堵的原因不同。** 1986 年互联网崩溃来自**数据重发**；车不会重发，只是面对面顶住。

*【指底部一行】* So we skip methods made for resending. Robots are closer to a **lossless network**, one that never drops data.

> *【指底部一行】*所以对付重发的办法不借。车更接近**无损网络**，也就是从不丢数据的网络。

> *【指底部 11】*我们后来试的 **11 种换路办法确实全部变差**。真正的问题是**派多少车、哪边先走**，这是调度。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: a per-door quota, like a TCP window

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:20　·　配图：图 9 在途车与过门数；图 8 名额公式**

**提示卡**

- TCP window: sent but not acknowledged
- We count: holds a door-6 order, not crossed, even far away
- Full → assign an order that does not cross
- 2.23 × 247 ÷ 500 × 13 → 15 in, 10 out
- Nothing dropped, fixed → must test
- Red: 211 in flight, 3 cross; blue: 25 in flight, 26 cross

**讲稿**

Scheduling method one: a **quota per door**, borrowed from the **TCP window**. TCP limits data **sent but not yet confirmed**.

> 调度方法一：**每门名额**，借自 **TCP 窗口**。TCP 限制**发出去、还没确认**的数据。

We limit robots that **have a door-6 order but have not crossed yet**, even far away. *【指右边三条】* When the quota is full, the robot gets **an order that does not cross**.

> 我们限制**接了过门 6 的单、还没过门**的车，哪怕离门还很远。*【指右边三条】*名额满了，就给这台车派**一单不过门的**。

*【指黄色框】* The size is **bandwidth times delay**: **2.23 times 247 divided by 500 times 13**. That gives **15 in and 10 out**.

> *【指黄色框】*名额按**带宽乘时延**算：**2.23 × 247 ÷ 500 × 13**，得到**进 15 台、出 10 台**。

Unlike TCP, nothing is dropped, so we had to test it.

> 和 TCP 不同，这里不丢包，所以必须实验检验。

*【指左图红线】* In red, with no control, **211** robots head for the door and only **3** get through. *【指蓝点】* In blue, with the quota, about **25** head for it and **26** get through. **More robots do not go faster. They jam.**

> *【指左图红线】*红线不管门，**211 台**车奔向门，只过 **3 台**。*【指蓝点】*蓝点用名额，25 台左右奔向门，过 **26 台**。**车多了不会更快，只会堵死。**

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 2: one side at a time, switch when empty

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:15　·　配图：图 10 门口规则示意；图 11 门利用率公式**

**提示卡**

- Half-duplex + polling
- Same direction 9 ticks; switch 21 ticks
- Switch every robot: 9/21 = 43%
- Batches of 10: 90/102 = 88%
- Serve one side → drain → switch, no timer
- 4-cell zone: no network counterpart

**讲稿**

Method two: the **door rule**, borrowed from **half-duplex links and polling**, which means serving each side in turn.

> 方法二：**门口规则**，借自**半双工链路和轮询**，也就是两边轮流服务。

We measured: robots in the same direction follow every **9 ticks**, but switching direction costs **21 ticks**.

> 我们测了：同方向每 **9 拍**跟过一台，换方向要 **21 拍**。

*【指 43%】* Switch after every robot, and the door is busy only **9 out of 21: 43 percent**. *【指 88%】* Let ten through each time, and it is **90 out of 102: 88 percent**.

> *【指 43%】*每过一台就换向，门只有 **9 ÷ 21 = 43%** 的时间在过车。*【指 88%】*每次放 10 台，是 **90 ÷ 102 = 88%**。

*【指上面三格图，从左到右】* So the door serves one side until it is empty, then switches. **No timer needed.**

> *【指上面三格图，从左到右】*所以门先服务一边，走空了再换向，**不需要定时器**。

*【指 4 cells】* One rule is **not from networks**: robots on the other side **step back four cells** to keep the exit clear, because **a waiting robot blocks the way**.

> *【指 4 cells】*有一条**网络里没有**：对面的车**退出门口 4 格**，把出口让出来，因为**等着的车会挡路**。

## 第 12 页　A bug fix and two safety nets

![第 12 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-12.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:10　·　配图：图 12 执行器修复示意；图 13 全图上限公式**

**提示卡**

- Stuck at 22
- Credit flow control: return a freed slot at once
- Executor kept robot in old cell's queue
- 3 lines → 26
- Global cap 138 (Little's law)
- Watchdog 300 ticks; relax rule, never drop
- Hand over to Speaker 4

**讲稿**

Third, a **bug fix**. Door 6 was **stuck at 22** robots per 500 ticks. In **credit flow control**, a freed slot must be reported **at once**.

> 第三，**修一个程序错误**。门 6 **卡在每 500 拍 22 台**。**信用流控**要求空位一腾出来就**立刻**报告。

*【指上排】* But the official executor **kept a robot in a cell's queue after it left**, so the next robot waited a whole round.

> *【指上排】*但官方执行器**在车离开后，那一格的排队名单里还留着它**，后面的车白等一整轮。

*【指下排和 22 → 26】* **Three lines** fixed it: back to **26**.

> *【指下排和 22 → 26】*改了 **3 行**，回到 **26 台**。

*【指右上】* Then, **two safety nets**. A **global cap** from Little's law: **1.76 times 69.2 divided by 500, times 2.23, times 255, is 138 robots** with orders at most.

> *【指右上】*然后是**两个兜底**。按 Little 定律的**全图上限**：**1.76 × 69.2 ÷ 500 × 2.23 × 255 = 138 台**，同时接单最多 138 台。

*【指右下】* And a **deadlock watchdog**: if a robot waits **300 ticks** and its door is idle that long, it may skip the door rule for one tick. **Networks drop packets here; we relax the rule instead.**

> *【指右下】*还有**死锁看门狗**：车等满 **300 拍**、它的门也 300 拍没车进，就允许它这一拍不守门口规则。**网络在这时丢包，我们改成放宽规则。**
