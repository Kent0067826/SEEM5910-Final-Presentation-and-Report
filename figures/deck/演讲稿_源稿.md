## 第 5 页　Prior work: four groups of methods

![第 5 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-05.png)

**讲者 2　·　0:40　·　配图：无（文字和数字卡片）**

**提示卡**

- 4 groups: assignment / route guidance / planners (deadlocks & collisions) / jam handling
- Each fixes one kind of congestion locally
- None avoids congestion at assignment level, whole system
- Our question: a global scheduling algorithm?

**讲稿**

*【依次指四张卡】* Earlier methods fall into four groups.

> *【依次指四张卡】*前人的方法可以分成四类。

**Order assignment** gives robots close, cheap orders.

> **派单**给车分近的、便宜的单。

**Route guidance** makes busy roads cost more, so robots spread out.

> **选路**让拥挤的路代价更高，把车分散开。

**Planners** decide which robot moves first, and avoid **deadlocks and collisions**.

> **规划**决定哪台车先走，避免**死锁和碰撞**。

**Jam handling** keeps some robots idle, or sorts out robots that meet in a narrow passage.

> **拥堵处理**让一部分车闲着，或者处理在窄道里碰上的车。

*【指底部黄框】* In all the methods we read, each one only fixes **one kind of congestion locally**. None starts from **the whole system** and weighs many factors to **avoid congestion when orders are given out**. So we wanted to try: is there a **global scheduling algorithm** that avoids congestion?

> *【指底部黄框】*在我们读到的所有方法里，每一种都只是**单独在局部优化某种拥塞**，没有一种从**整个系统**出发、同时考虑多个因素**在派单层面去避免拥塞**，所以我们想进行这个尝试，看看有没有一种**全局调度算法**去避免拥塞。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: a window for each door

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:05　·　配图：窗口大小原理图（从“路上的车数 = 过门速率 × 路程时间”推出；这条计数规律在排队论里叫 Little 定律，讲稿不提名字，只写在图注里；理论示意，附门 6 的数字）**

**提示卡**

- Our measure: robots on the way to a door (hold an order through it, not crossed yet)
- Window (quota) = how many orders may cross a door at once
- Door 6: 1 robot per 38.5 ticks; trip 550 ticks → 550 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3 → window 15
- Rule: on the way = door rate × trip time
- Fewer: door waits; more: queue; even more: block the other side → rate falls

**讲稿**

How many robots should head for door 6 at once? We came up with a way to **measure how many robots are on the way** to a door: robots that hold an order through it but have not crossed yet. This number is our **window**: it decides **how many orders may cross the door at the same time**.

> 同时该有多少车奔向门 6？我们想到一个办法：**衡量在途、会通过某个门口的车有多少**，也就是接了要过这道门的单、还没过门的车。这个数就是我们的**窗口**，用它来决定**同时指派多少个要穿过这道门的任务**。

How big should the window be? Door 6 lets in one robot about every **38.5 ticks** in each direction, and a robot needs about **550 ticks** to get there, breakdowns included. To keep the door busy, we send one every 38.5 ticks, so about **550 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3** robots are on the way at any moment: the window is **15**. In general, robots on the way = **door rate × trip time**.

> 窗口该多大？门 6 每个方向大约**每 38.5 拍进一台**，一台车走到门口大约要 **550 拍**（算上故障）。要让门一直有车过，就每 38.5 拍派出一台，所以任何时候路上大约有 **550 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3 台**：窗口取 **15**。一般地说，路上的车数 = **过门速率 × 路程时间**。

*【指左图上半】* Fewer than that, and the door **waits**; more, and the extras just *【指左图下半】* **queue**. Even more, and waiting robots **block the other side**, so the rate **falls**.

> *【指左图上半】*比这少，门就**等车**；比这多，多出来的车只会*【指左图下半】***排队**。再多，等着的车还会**挡住对面**，速率反而**下降**。

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 1: global regulation, like BBR

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:05　·　配图：BBR 控制环与 DoorFlow 对照图（含第 50 轮实测时延比）**

**提示卡**

- Goal: global regulation; a buffer size (quota) per door & direction
- Grow until something breaks? robots never lost → no stop signal → queue grows
- BBR = how Google's servers pace internet traffic: max delivery rate, min round trip; the min keeps a jam from raising its own limit
- Our delay: no queue = shortest-path time; real time (on the way ÷ rate); missed spots 2.2–2.9× → a fixed formula there holds back doors that are not jammed
- BBR-style cap (round 51): bites only when delay rises and crossings fall; big losses gone, overall +0.08% [−0.32, +0.47] → kept the fixed quota

**讲稿**

Our goal is **global regulation**: a **buffer size**, the quota, for every door and direction at once.

> 我们的目标是**全局调控**：同时给每道门、每个方向一个**缓冲区大小**，也就是名额。

Growing the window until something breaks does not work here: robots are never lost, so there is **no stop signal**, and the queue just grows.

> 让窗口一直变大、直到出问题才停，在这里行不通：车永远不会丢，**没有“停”的信号**，队只会越排越长。

*【指左图第 2 框】* So we borrowed an idea from **BBR**, the method Google's servers use to set how fast to send data. It **measures** the **highest delivery rate** and the **shortest delay** it has seen, since queuing only adds delay. *【指右上橙框】* Following the current delay would let a jam raise its own limit.

> *【指左图第 2 框】*所以我们借了 **BBR** 的思路。BBR 是谷歌的服务器决定数据发多快的方法：它**去测**看到过的**最大送达速率**和**最短时延**，因为排队只会增加时延。*【指右上橙框】*如果跟着当前时延走，堵车就会把自己的上限抬高。

*【指下方对照表】* We do the same: our no-queue delay is the **shortest-path time**. *【指右下柱状图】* But on spots our first model missed, the real time was **2.2 to 2.9 times** the model, so a fixed formula there would **hold back doors that were not jammed**.

> *【指下方对照表】*我们也这样做：不排队时的时延就是**最短路时间**。*【指右下柱状图】*但在第一版模型漏掉的窄处，真实时间是模型的 **2.2 到 2.9 倍**，所以在那里用固定公式，会**卡住本来不堵的门**。

So there we tried a **BBR-style cap**. It removed big losses where a door collapsed, but overall it added only **0.08 percent**, so we kept the fixed quota.

> 所以在这些窄处，我们试了 **BBR 式上限**。它把门塌掉时的大损失补回来了，但总体只多了 **0.08%**，所以保留固定名额。

## 第 12 页　Scheduling 1: the quota for each door

![第 12 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-12.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:40　·　配图：图 9 在途车与过门数；图 8 名额公式**

**提示卡**

- Count: holds a door-6 order, not crossed, even far away; full → order that does not cross
- One window for all (round 25): +0.53% on the champion (small)
- Per door & direction (rounds 28–29) → +29.1% alone (17 room-map instances)
- Size: 8 too tight, 32+ jams → formula 15 in / 10 out (round 41), no worse than hand-tuned (+0.63%)
- No control 211 → 3; quota ~25 (knee) → 26

**讲稿**

When a door's quota is full, the next robot gets **an order that does not need the door**.

> 某道门的名额满了，下一台车就派**一单不用过这道门的**。

*【指右边第二条】* Counting all robots together gained only **0.53 percent**. *【指右边第三条】* Counting **per door and per direction** gained **29.1 percent** on its own, on 17 room-map instances.

> *【指右边第二条】*把所有车一起数，只多了 **0.53%**。*【指右边第三条】*改成**按门、按方向**数，单独就多了 **29.1%**（17 个房间图实例）。

*【指公式】* Hand-tuned sizes were too tight at 8 and jammed at 32 or more; the formula gives **15 in** and **10 out**, as good as the best hand-tuned value.

## 第 14 页　Scheduling 2: how big should the buffer be?

![第 14 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-14.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:55　·　配图：门口规则详图 B、C：缓冲区大小扫描（12 个开发实例）；门 6 每过一台 18.7 拍的分解（第 52 轮 669 次过门）；下限 ⌈9 ÷ 3⌉ = 3 格的公式见 netmac.cpp 第 59–62 行注释（第 57 轮）**

**提示卡**

- Buffer minimum: 9 ticks ÷ 3 ticks per move = 3 cells
- Sweep (12 dev, vs 4): 1 cell −9.0%, 2 −5.2%, 3 even (confirm −2.64%, deadlocks 11 → 29), 6 −4.2% → 4 = minimum + 1
- 18.7 ticks per crossing (round 52); breakdowns 4.8; 500 ÷ 18.7 ≈ 26.7 ≈ 26

**讲稿**

*【指上图 B】* How big should the buffer be? A formula gives the minimum: robots come out every **9 ticks** and one move takes **3 ticks**, so a leaving robot must get **9 ÷ 3 = 3 cells** away before the next one. We tested 1 to 6 cells on **12 development instances**: 1 and 2 cells lost **9.0 and 5.2 percent**; 3 cells looked even, but a confirmation test lost **2.64 percent**, with deadlocked runs up from **11 to 29**; 6 cells lost **4.2 percent**, as the queue blocked other doors. > *【指上图 B】*缓冲区该多大？先用公式算下限：同方向每 **9 拍**出来一台，车走一步要 **3 拍**，所以刚出门的车要在下一台出来前走出 **9 ÷ 3 = 3 格**。我们在 **12 个开发实例**上试了 1 到 6 格：1 格、2 格分别少 **9.0%、5.2%**；3 格看起来持平，但确认测试少 **2.64%**，堵死的局从 **11 局增到 29 局**；6 格少 **4.2%**，因为排队的车挡住了别的门。所以用 **4 格**：下限再多留 1 格。

*【指下图 C】* The buffer also keeps **random pauses** out of the exit: each robot needs **18.7 ticks** at door 6, **4.8** of them breakdowns. And **500 ÷ 18.7 ≈ 26.7**: that is where the door's **26** comes from.

> *【指下图 C】*缓冲区也让**随机暂停**不会堵在出口：门 6 每过一台要 **18.7 拍**，其中 **4.8 拍**是故障。而 **500 ÷ 18.7 ≈ 26.7**，这就是门每 500 拍过 **26 台**的来历。

## 第 15 页　Scheduling 2: queue scheduling at the door

![第 15 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-15.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:55　·　配图：门利用率曲线（演讲专用：U(n) = 9n / (9(n−1) + 21)，h、s 为实测值）**

**提示卡**

- Door = server with 2 queues, one per direction
- One turn of n robots: 21 + 9(n − 1) ticks; without switching 9n
- U(n) = 9n ÷ (21 + 9(n − 1)): n = 1 → 43%, n = 10 → 88%
- Tests: fixed timetable −5.0%, −6.4%; until empty vs ≤ 80 steps: +1.1% (mostly new room maps)

**讲稿**

The door is like a **server with two queues**, one per direction: which queue should it serve, and for how long?

> 门就像一个**有两个队列的服务台**，每个方向一个队列：先服务哪个队列、服务多久？

*【指公式】* Here is the formula. In one turn, the door lets **n** robots through in one direction. The first needs **21 ticks**, because the door must empty and change direction; each next one needs **9**. So a turn takes **21 + 9 × (n − 1)** ticks, where **9 × n** would be enough without switching: the door runs at **U(n) = 9n ÷ (21 + 9(n − 1))** of its top speed. *【指曲线左端】* One robot per turn: **9 ÷ 21 = 43 percent**; *【指 n = 10】* ten per turn: **90 ÷ 102 = 88 percent**.

> *【指公式】*公式是这样来的：门一轮放同一方向的 **n** 台车。换向后的第一台要 **21 拍**，因为门要先清空、再换方向；之后每台要 **9 拍**。所以一轮要 **21 + 9 × (n − 1)** 拍，而不换向时 **9 × n** 拍就够：门的速度是最快速度的 **U(n) = 9n ÷ (21 + 9(n − 1))**。*【指曲线左端】*每轮只放 1 台：**9 ÷ 21 = 43%**；*【指 n = 10】*每轮放 10 台：**90 ÷ 102 = 88%**。

So we serve one side **until it is empty**. We tested this too: switching on a fixed timetable lost **5.0 and 6.4 percent**, and serving until empty beat a limit of 80 steps per turn by **1.1 percent**, mostly on new room maps.

> 所以让一边**走空再换**。这一点我们也测过：按固定时间表换向少了 **5.0% 和 6.4%**；走空再换比每轮最多放 80 步多 **1.1%**，主要来自新生成的房间图。
