[Thank you. So why did changing routes not help? First, we built a model.]{.underline} *【指左图】* The **16 rooms are nodes** and the **31 doors are edges**; *【指红线门 6】* **door 6 is a cut**: without it, the four lower-left rooms are cut off.

> 谢谢。那为什么改路线没有用？我们先建了一个模型。*【指左图】***16 个房间是点，31 道门是线**；*【指红线门 6】***门 6 是一个割**：没有它，左下 4 个房间就断开了。

> *【指右 1】*左下房间占 3614 个房间格里的 **906 个**，**906 ÷ 3614 ≈ 0.2507**，约四分之一。

> *【指右 2】*如果取货点和送货点随机落在地图上，一单两头落在门的两侧才要过门 6，概率是 **2 × 0.2507 × 0.7493 ≈ 37.6%**；任务文件里是 38.5%。

> *【指公式】*门 6 每 500 拍过 **26 台**：我们给过门计时，平均**每 18.7 拍过一台**，**500 ÷ 18.7 ≈ 26.7**。所以不管车怎么走，整张图每 500 拍最多完成 **26 ÷ 0.376 ≈ 69 单**。

> 所以我们比较过的 15 种换路办法里有 **14 种**都变差。真正的问题是**派多少车、哪边先走**，也就是**调度**。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: a window for each door

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:51　·　配图：窗口大小原理图（从“路上的车数 = 过门速率 × 路程时间”推出；这条计数规律在排队论里叫 Little 定律，讲稿不提名字，只写在图注里；理论示意）**

**提示卡**

- Our measure: robots on the way to a door (hold an order through it, not crossed yet) = window
- Door takes one robot every r ticks, trip T ticks → T ÷ r on the way → window = rate × trip time
- Fewer: door waits; more: queue, block the other side

**讲稿**

[How many robots should head for door 6 at once?]{.underline} We came up with a way to **measure how many robots are on the way** to a door: robots that hold an order through it but have not crossed yet. This number is our **window**: it decides **how many orders may cross the door at the same time**.

> 同时该有多少车奔向门 6？我们想到一个办法：**衡量在途、会通过某个门口的车有多少**，也就是接了要过这道门的单、还没过门的车。这个数就是我们的**窗口**，用它来决定**同时指派多少个要穿过这道门的任务**。

[How big should the window be?]{.underline} If the door takes one robot every **r** ticks and a trip takes **T** ticks, then to keep the door busy, the robots sent during the last T ticks must all be on the way: **T ÷ r** of them. [So]{.underline} **window = door rate × trip time**.

> 窗口该多大？如果门每 **r** 拍放进一台，一趟路要 **T** 拍，那么要让门一直有车过，过去 T 拍里派出的车都得还在路上，也就是 **T ÷ r** 台。所以**窗口 = 过门速率 × 路程时间**。

*【指左图上半】* Fewer robots, and the door **waits**; *【指左图下半】* more, and the extras only **queue** and **block the other side**.

> *【指左图上半】*车少了，门就**等车**；*【指左图下半】*车多了，多出来的只会**排队**，还会**挡住对面**。

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 1: door 6's window, number by number

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:53　·　配图：门 6 窗口的推算表（幻灯片上直接画的；复算脚本 figures/deck/demo/door6_numbers.py，结果在 door6_numbers.json）**

**提示卡**

- Rate: counted in runs, 26 / 500 ticks; 620 in / 635 out → 13 each way → 38.5 ticks per robot
- Orders random → average: shortest time from every far-side cell (3 ticks per step/turn) + 25 to pickup → 247 / 158
- 2.23 measured (round 40: order → door, 224 cases); breakdowns off: 1.33 → breakdowns cause most of the extra time
- 2.23 × 247 ≈ 551 → 551 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3 → 15; 2.23 × 158 ≈ 352 → 9.2 → 10
- Planner prints 247 / 158, 15 / 10 (ROBOT_BRIDGE_TRACE); next slide: why averages

**讲稿**

*【指第一行】* The door rate is **measured** by counting crossings: **26 robots** per 500 ticks, half each way (**620 in, 635 out**), so **13 each way**: one every **500 ÷ 13 ≈ 38.5 ticks**.

> *【指第一行】*过门速率是**实测**的：在仿真里数过门的车，每 500 拍 **26 台**，两个方向各一半（**进 620 台、出 635 台**），所以每个方向 **13 台**，也就是**每 500 ÷ 13 ≈ 38.5 拍一台**。

*【指第二行】* Orders appear at random, so we **average**: the shortest time from **every cell** on the far side to the door, at 3 ticks per step or turn, plus about 25 ticks to the pickup: **247 ticks** inward, **158** outward.

> *【指第二行】*任务是随机出现的，所以用**平均值**：程序把门另一侧**每一格**到门口的最短时间都算出来（走一步或转一次都是 3 拍），取平均，再加上走到取货点的约 25 拍：进左下 **247 拍**，出左下 **158 拍**。

*【指第三行】* Real trips took **2.23 times** as long, timed from getting the order to reaching the door; with breakdowns turned off, only **1.33 times**. [So]{.underline} breakdowns cause most of the extra time.

> *【指第三行】*实际路程是空路的 **2.23 倍**，这是给车计时测的，从接单一直到走到门口；把故障关掉再测，只有 **1.33 倍**。所以多出来的时间大部分是故障造成的。

*【指最后两行】* [So]{.underline} the real trip is **2.23 × 247 ≈ 551 ticks**, and the window is **551 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3**, rounded up to **15**; outward, **352 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 9.2**, so **10**.

> *【指最后两行】*所以实际路程是 **2.23 × 247 ≈ 551 拍**，窗口 = **551 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 14.3**，向上取整得 **15**；出左下方向 **352 ÷ 38.5 ≈ 9.2**，得 **10**。

## 第 12 页　Scheduling 1: why a fixed window from averages?

![第 12 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-12.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:42　·　配图：边看边调的三种做法重跑对比（演讲专用：`demo/live_control.py`，官方房间图 200 / 250 / 300 台 × 20 对种子，每种做法 60 局，和同种子的 DoorFlow 比；上图是一局里往左下去的车数，下图是 60 局的平均差别）**

**提示卡**

- Orders random → only the average trip is stable; the window counts many robots, so the average is enough; counting is live
- Reran 3 live ideas on today's DoorFlow, 60 paired runs each (official map, 20 seed pairs × 3 sizes)
- Limit only after a jam is seen: −1.1% (too late: robots sent ~550 ticks earlier are already on the way)
- Adjust by measured delay (AIMD): −3.3%, window swings 8–41
- Physical capacity 49 instead of measured 26: −7.5%

**讲稿**

[Why averages, if orders are random?]{.underline} The window only counts **how many robots are on the way**, and that depends on the **average** trip, which is stable. The counting is **live**, every round.

> 任务是随机的，为什么用平均数？窗口只管**同时有多少台车在路上**，这只取决于**平均**路程，而平均数是稳定的。数车是**实时**的，每一轮都数。

[Could we adjust it live instead?]{.underline} We reran three ideas from our log on today's DoorFlow, **60 paired runs** each.

> 能不能把窗口也做成边看边调？我们把记录里的三种做法加到现在的 DoorFlow 上重跑，每种 **60 局**，和同种子的 DoorFlow 成对比较。

*【指红线】* Limiting only after a jam is seen: no better, **−1.1 percent**; by then, robots sent in the last **550 ticks** are already on the way.

## 第 16 页　Scheduling 2: serve one side until it is empty

**讲者 3　·　0:47　·　配图：门速度曲线（演讲专用：U(n) = nh / ((n−1)h + s)，h = 10、s = 34 用上一页的实测中位数）**
