## 第 1 页　Managing Warehouse Robots Like a Computer Network

**讲者 1　·　0:20　·　配图：无（文字和数字卡片）**

**English.** Good afternoon. Our project asks a simple question: when hundreds of robots share a warehouse with narrow doors, how do we keep them from jamming? Our answer is to manage the warehouse the way computer networks manage traffic.

**中文对照.** 下午好。我们的项目问一个简单的问题：几百台机器人在有窄门的仓库里一起干活，怎么不让它们堵死？我们的答案是：像计算机网络管流量那样管仓库。

## 第 2 页　The task: hundreds of robots, narrow doors

![第 2 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-02.png)

**讲者 1　·　0:50　·　配图：图 1 背景图**

**English.** This is the League of Robot Runners, a competition sponsored by Amazon Robotics, now in its third season. Robots keep picking up and delivering orders for five thousand ticks, and the score is how many orders they finish. Each tick, the program makes three decisions: which robot takes which order, which route it follows, and whether its next step can go now. We work on the official room map: sixteen rooms and thirty-one doors, most of them one cell wide, with two hundred to three hundred robots. A single order, like this blue one, can cross eight doors.

**中文对照.** 这是亚马逊机器人赞助的机器人跑者联赛，今年是第三届。机器人在 5000 拍里不停取货送货，比的是完成多少单。程序每拍做三个决定：哪台车接哪单、走哪条路、下一步现在能不能走。我们做的是官方房间图：16 个房间、31 道门，大多只有一格宽，200 到 300 台车。像图上这一单，要穿过 8 道门。

## 第 3 页　The longer it runs, the slower it gets

![第 3 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-03.png)

**讲者 1　·　0:50　·　配图：图 2 越跑越慢曲线**

**English.** We ran the official starter program and the 2024 champion's task assignment. Something strange happened: the longer the run, the slower it got. With the champion's assignment, completions per five hundred ticks fell from 127 at the start to 37 at the end, averaged over sixty runs. There were no errors and no collisions. Every robot was following its plan.

**中文对照.** 我们跑了官方起始程序和 2024 届冠军的派单。奇怪的是：跑得越久越慢。冠军派单下，60 局平均，每 500 拍的完成数从开局 127 单降到最后 37 单。没有报错，没有碰撞，每台车都在按计划走。

## 第 4 页　Robots jam at one narrow door

![第 4 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-04.png)

**讲者 1　·　0:50　·　配图：图 3 第 3000 拍堵门截图**

**English.** So we watched the replay tick by tick. At tick three thousand, ninety-seven robots are waiting to cross one door, door six, into the lower-left rooms. The easy orders get done first, so the share of orders that must cross this door grows from 38 percent to as much as 86. Robots meet face to face in a one-cell door. In the second half, the door passes only 3.4 to 7 robots per five hundred ticks, while an orderly door passes 26. More robots, fewer crossings.

**中文对照.** 于是我们逐拍看回放。第 3000 拍，97 台车在等着穿过同一道门，也就是门 6，进左下房间。好做的单先做完，要过这道门的单从 38% 涨到最多 86%。车在一格宽的门口面对面顶住。后半程门每 500 拍只过 3.4 到 7 台，而门口有序时能过 26 台。车越多，过得越少。

## 第 5 页　Prior work misses one number

![第 5 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-05.png)

**讲者 1　·　0:55　·　配图：无（文字和数字卡片）**

**English.** People have worked on this for years, and prior methods fall into four groups. Task assignment picks nearby, cheap orders. Route guidance adds congestion costs so traffic spreads to other roads. Planners such as PIBT decide who moves first and avoid collisions. Congestion handling lets only part of the fleet be active. Each does its own step well. But the lower-left rooms have only one door, so spreading traffic cannot help, and the starter program already includes route guidance and still slows down. In the methods we read, no one counts, at assignment time, how many robots already hold an order that must cross this door in this direction.

**中文对照.** 这个问题前人研究了很多年，大致分四类：派单挑近的、便宜的单；选路给拥挤的路加代价，让车流分散；PIBT 这类规划决定谁先走、避免碰撞；拥堵处理只让一部分车同时活动。它们各自那一步都做得很好。但左下房间只有一道门，分流没有用；官方起始程序本身就带交通流引导，照样变慢。在我们读到的方法里，没有一种在派单时去数：已经接了要往这个方向过这道门的单的车有多少。

## 第 6 页　Our idea: treat the warehouse as a computer network

![第 6 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-06.png)

**讲者 2　·　1:00　·　配图：无（文字和数字卡片）**

**English.** Looking at the replay, the warehouse reminded us of a computer network. A door is like a link with limited bandwidth. An order crossing several doors is like a packet crossing several routers. And more traffic giving less throughput looks like network congestion. We chose computer networks for three reasons. The field has solved the problem of many senders sharing limited links for decades. It is mature, with many proven routing and scheduling algorithms. And we are computer science students who know these algorithms and their assumptions. So our hypothesis is: borrow routing and scheduling methods from computer networks, and the fleet may finish more orders. Whether that is true, and which kind helps, had to be tested.

**中文对照.** 看着回放，我们觉得仓库像一张计算机网络：门像带宽有限的链路；一单要过好几道门，像数据包经过好几台路由器；车越多过得越少，像网络拥塞。选计算机网络有三个原因：它几十年来一直在解决很多发送者共用有限线路的问题；它很成熟，有大量经过检验的路由和调度算法；我们是计算机专业的学生，熟悉这些算法和它们的前提。所以我们的猜想是：借用网络的路由和调度方法，车队也许能完成更多的单。对不对、哪一类有用，要靠实验。

## 第 7 页　The model: 16 rooms, 31 links, routers at the doors

![第 7 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-07.png)

**讲者 2　·　1:00　·　配图：图 4 地图压成网络**

**English.** First we built the model. We collapsed 3,646 cells into a network of 16 rooms and 31 doors. Door six stands out at once: it is a cut. Remove it, and the lower-left rooms are cut off. Then we mapped each part. The assignment center is the sender, a robot is a packet, a door is a half-duplex link, and the area within eight steps of a door is the router port. We put the router there because of measured data: one to three steps from a door, robots spend 4.2 times as long stopped as moving, and the door cell plus two steps around it hold 30 percent of all waiting on 10 percent of the area.

**中文对照.** 我们先建模型。把 3646 个格子压成 16 个房间、31 道门的网络。门 6 马上显出来：它是一个割，拿掉它左下房间就断开了。然后逐项对应：派单中心是发送端，机器人是数据包，门是半双工链路，门口 8 步以内是路由器端口。路由器放在门口是因为实测：离门 1 到 3 步，车停着的时间是走的 4.2 倍；门格和两侧 2 步只占 10% 面积，却装了 30% 的等待。

## 第 8 页　Similar, but not the same

![第 8 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-08.png)

**讲者 2　·　0:50　·　配图：图 5 仓库与网络的对应**

**English.** But similar is not the same, and there are four differences. Robots cannot be dropped; they can only wait. A waiting robot occupies a cell and may block the other side. The fleet size is fixed. And the cause of jams differs: the 1986 internet collapse came from retransmissions, while here nothing is retransmitted; robots simply meet face to face. So we skip methods built for retransmission, and we keep in mind that robots are closer to a lossless data-center network.

**中文对照.** 但像不等于一样，有四点不同：机器人不能丢，只能等；等的车占着格子，可能挡住对面；车数固定；堵的原因也不同，1986 年互联网崩溃来自重传，这里没有重传，是车面对面顶住。所以对付重传的办法不借，并且记住机器人更接近数据中心的无损网络。

## 第 9 页　Routing or scheduling? The ceiling decides

![第 9 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-09.png)

**讲者 2　·　0:55　·　配图：图 7 天花板公式**

**English.** Routing or scheduling? We tried routing first, and it did not help. The ceiling explains why. The lower-left rooms hold 906 of 3,614 room cells, or 25 percent. An order needs door six if its two ends fall on different sides: two times 0.25 times 0.75, which is 37.6 percent; the task file gives 38.5. The door passes 26 robots per five hundred ticks, so the map can finish at most 26 divided by 0.376, which is 69 orders. Door six alone sets this ceiling, so rerouting cannot raise it. Later we tried eleven rerouting methods, and all were worse. The real question is how many robots to send, and which side goes first. That is scheduling.

**中文对照.** 改路由还是改调度？我们先试路由，没用。天花板解释了原因：左下房间占 3614 个房间格里的 906 个，即 25%。一单两头落在两侧就要过门 6：2 × 0.25 × 0.75 = 37.6%，任务文件实测 38.5%。门每 500 拍过 26 台，所以整张图最多 26 ÷ 0.376 = 69 单。只有门 6 决定这个上限，换路提高不了。后来试了 11 种换路办法，全部变差。真正的问题是派多少车、先放哪边，这是调度。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: a per-door quota, like a TCP window

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:20　·　配图：图 9 在途车与过门数；图 8 名额公式**

**English.** The first scheduling method is a per-door quota, borrowed from the TCP window. TCP limits data that is sent but not yet acknowledged. We limit robots that hold an order needing door six but have not crossed yet, even if they are still far away. When the quota is full, the assigner gives that robot an order that does not cross the door. The quota comes from bandwidth times delay: 2.23 for breakdowns, times 247 ticks to reach the door, times 13 robots per five hundred ticks per direction, gives 15 inbound and 10 outbound. Unlike TCP, nothing is dropped and the quota is fixed, so it had to be tested. In red, without control, robots in flight climb to 211 and only 3 cross. In blue, with the quota, 25 are in flight and the door passes 26 on average.

**中文对照.** 第一个调度方法是每门名额，借自 TCP 窗口。TCP 限制已发出、还没确认的数据；我们限制已经接了要过门 6 的单、还没过门的车，哪怕它还很远。名额满了，就给这台车派一单不过门的。名额按带宽乘时延算：2.23 的故障放慢 × 247 拍走到门口 × 每方向每 500 拍 13 台，得到进 15 台、出 10 台。和 TCP 不同，这里不丢包、名额固定，所以必须实验。红线是不管门：在途车涨到 211 台，只过 3 台；蓝点是用名额：在途 25 台，门平均过 26 台。

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 2: one side at a time, switch when empty

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:15　·　配图：图 10 门口规则示意；图 11 门利用率公式**

**English.** The second method is the door rule, from half-duplex links and polling. In the same direction, robots follow every 9 ticks; switching direction takes 21 ticks. If the door switches after every robot, it is busy only 9 over 21, or 43 percent of the time. With batches of 10, it is 90 over 102, or 88 percent. So the door serves one side until that side is empty, then switches, with no timer. One more rule has no network counterpart: robots on the other side back out of a four-cell zone to keep the exit clear, because a waiting robot blocks the way and a waiting packet does not.

**中文对照.** 第二个方法是门口规则，借自半双工链路和轮询。同方向每 9 拍跟过一台，换向要 21 拍。每过一台就换向，门只有 9/21 = 43% 的时间在用；一批放 10 台是 90/102 = 88%。所以门服务一边直到这一边走空才换，不用定时器。还有一条网络里没有：对面的车退出 4 格门口区，让出出口，因为等着的车会挡路，等着的数据包不会。

## 第 12 页　A bug fix and two safety nets

![第 12 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-12.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:10　·　配图：图 12 执行器修复示意；图 13 全图上限公式**

**English.** Third, a bug fix. Even with both rules, the door was stuck at 22. Thinking in credit flow control, where a freed slot must be returned at once, we found that the official executor kept a robot in a cell's queue after it had left. Three lines fixed it, and the door went back to 26. Finally, two safety nets. A global cap from Little's law: 1.76 times 69.2 over 500, times 2.23, times 255, gives 138 robots holding orders at once. And a deadlock watchdog from lossless data-center networks: if a robot has waited 300 ticks and its door has had no entry for 300 ticks, it may ignore the door rule for one tick. Switches drop packets to recover; we cannot drop robots, so we relax the rule instead.

**中文对照.** 第三是修一个程序错误。两条规则都上了，门还卡在 22 台。按信用流控“空位腾出就立刻归还”的思路去查，发现官方执行器在车离开一格后，排队记录里还留着它。改 3 行，门回到 26 台。最后是两个兜底：按 Little 定律的全图上限，1.76 × 69.2 / 500 × 2.23 × 255 = 138 台车同时接单；以及借自无损网络的死锁看门狗：车等满 300 拍、所在门 300 拍没车进，就允许它这一拍不守门口规则。交换机靠丢包恢复，我们不能丢车，所以改成放宽规则。

## 第 14 页　What matters, and does it work on other maps?

![第 14 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-14.png)

**讲者 4　·　0:55　·　配图：图 16 逐项拿掉与加上**

**English.** Which parts matter? We re-ran six hundred games. Removing the door rule costs 25.6 percent, the quota 13.6, and the executor fix 6.7. The door rule or the quota alone gives about 29 percent; together they give 76. The quota controls how many robots come; the door rule controls how they pass. We also worried the method fits only this one map, so we generated eight new room maps with random doors. The full method wins on every one, by 86.9 percent on average. The global cap shows no effect on the official map, but on one generated map, removing it costs 16.9 percent. That is why we keep the safety nets.

**中文对照.** 哪些起作用？重跑 600 局：拿掉门口规则少 25.6%，名额少 13.6%，执行器修复少 6.7%。门口规则或名额单独用各约 29%，一起用 76%。名额管来多少，门口规则管怎么过。我们还担心方法只适合这一张图，于是随机生成 8 张新房间图：完整方法每张都赢，平均 +86.9%。全图上限在官方图上测不出作用，但在一张生成图上拿掉它少 16.9%，所以兜底要留。

## 第 15 页　Most ideas failed, and why

![第 15 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-15.png)

**讲者 4　·　0:50　·　配图：图 18 69 项机制的筛选漏斗**

**English.** Most ideas failed. In the protocol phase we wrote down 69 network mechanisms: 38 stopped at a first measurement, 23 failed development, 7 failed on unseen maps, and only the watchdog passed. Every rerouting method lost. The lessons: protocol assumptions often break on robots, which cannot be dropped and occupy cells; throughput is set by door six; and gains on development maps must be confirmed on new ones.

**中文对照.** 大多数想法失败了。协议阶段写下 69 种网络机制：38 种量一下就放弃，23 种开发没过，7 种在新图上没确认，只有看门狗通过。所有换路办法都变差。教训：协议的前提在机器人上常常不成立，因为不能丢、占格子；完成数由门 6 决定；开发集上的提升要换图确认。

## 第 16 页　Conclusion

![第 16 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-16.png)

**讲者 4　·　0:50　·　配图：无（文字和数字卡片）**

**English.** To conclude: we did not make each robot smarter. We managed the warehouse like a network, controlling how many robots head for a door and which side the door serves. Our hypothesis was half right: routing did not transfer, but scheduling did. In a real warehouse with a narrow aisle, this means a quota per direction, counted at assignment time, and one direction at a time at the aisle. All results come from simulation, and the parameters must be re-measured for a new layout. Thank you.

**中文对照.** 总结：我们没有让每台车更聪明，而是像管网络一样，管住多少车奔向一道门、这道门先放哪边。我们的猜想对了一半：路由搬不过来，调度有效。在有窄巷的真实仓库里，就是每个方向一个名额、派单时就算，巷口一次放一个方向。所有结果都来自仿真，换布局要重新测参数。谢谢。
