## 第 7 页　The model: 16 rooms, 31 links, routers at the doors

![第 7 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-07.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:00　·　配图：图 4 地图压成网络**

**提示卡**

- 3,646 cells → 16 nodes, 31 links
- Door 6 is a cut
- Sender / packet / half-duplex link / router port
- Router at the door: 4.2×; 30% of waiting on 10% of area

**English.** Thank you. To borrow network methods, we first needed a model. *【指左图】* We collapsed the 3,646 walkable cells into a network: **16 rooms become nodes, and 31 doors become links**. *【指红线门 6】* One link stands out at once: **door six is a cut**. Remove it, and the four lower-left rooms are cut off from the rest. *【指右边四行】* Then we mapped each part: the **assignment center is the sender**, a **robot is a packet**, a **door is a half-duplex link**, and the area **within eight steps of a door is the router port**. *【指黄色框】* Why put the router at the door? Because of measured data. One to three steps from a door, robots spend **4.2 times** as long stopped as moving. The door cell and two steps around it hold **30 percent of all waiting on only 10 percent of the area**. The time is lost at the door, so the router goes there.

**中文对照.** 谢谢。要借网络的方法，先要有模型。*【指左图】*我们把 3646 个可走格子压成一张网络：**16 个房间是点，31 道门是线**。*【指红线门 6】*马上有一条线显出来：**门 6 是一个割**，拿掉它，左下 4 个房间就和其他房间断开了。*【指右边四行】*然后逐项对应：**派单中心是发送端**，**机器人是数据包**，**门是半双工链路**，**门口 8 步以内是路由器端口**。*【指黄色框】*为什么路由器放在门口？因为实测数据：离门 1 到 3 步，车停着的时间是走的 **4.2 倍**；门格和两侧 2 步**只占 10% 的面积，却装了 30% 的等待**。时间丢在门口，路由器就放在门口。

## 第 8 页　Similar, but not the same

![第 8 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-08.png)

**讲者 3　·　0:50　·　配图：图 5 仓库与网络的对应**

**提示卡**

- 1 cannot be dropped
- 2 waiting robot blocks a cell
- 3 fixed fleet
- 4 no retransmission (1986 vs face to face)
- Closer to a lossless data-center network

**English.** But similar is not the same. *【指右边四张卡】* There are four differences. **One: robots cannot be dropped**; a router can drop a packet, but a robot can only wait. **Two: a waiting robot occupies a cell** and may block the robot coming the other way. **Three: the fleet is fixed**; robots stay on the map after delivering. **Four: the cause of jams is different.** The 1986 internet collapse came from **retransmissions**; here nothing is retransmitted, robots simply meet face to face. *【指底部一行】* So we do not borrow methods built for retransmission, and we remember that robots are closer to a **lossless data-center network**.

**中文对照.** 但像不等于一样。*【指右边四张卡】*有四点不同。**第一，机器人不能丢**：路由器可以丢包，车只能等。**第二，等着的车占着格子**，可能挡住对面来的车。**第三，车数固定**：送完货车还在地图上。**第四，堵的原因不同**：1986 年互联网崩溃来自**重传**；这里没有重传，是车面对面顶住。*【指底部一行】*所以对付重传的办法不借，并且记住机器人更接近数据中心的**无损网络**。

## 第 10 页　Scheduling 1: a per-door quota, like a TCP window

![第 10 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-10.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:20　·　配图：图 9 在途车与过门数；图 8 名额公式**

**提示卡**

- TCP window: sent but not acknowledged
- We count: holds a door-6 order, not crossed, even far away
- Full → assign an order that does not cross
- 2.23 × 247 ÷ 500 × 13 → 15 in, 10 out
- Nothing dropped, fixed → must test
- Red: 211 in flight, 3 cross; blue: 25 in flight, 26 cross

**English.** Our first scheduling method is a **per-door quota**, borrowed from the **TCP window**. In TCP, the sender limits data that is **sent but not yet acknowledged**. We limit robots that **already hold an order needing door six but have not crossed yet**, even if they are still far away in another room. *【指右边三条】* When the quota is full, the assigner simply gives that robot **an order that does not cross the door**. *【指黄色框】* The quota size comes from **bandwidth times delay**: a breakdown slowdown of **2.23**, times **247 ticks** to reach the door, times **13 robots per 500 ticks** in each direction. That gives **15 inbound and 10 outbound**. Unlike TCP, **nothing is dropped and the quota is fixed**, so whether it works had to be tested. *【指左图红线】* Here is the test. In red, without control, robots in flight climb to **211**, and only **3** cross per 500 ticks. *【指蓝点】* In blue, with the quota, about **25** are in flight, and the door passes **26 on average**. Too many robots do not cross faster; **they jam**.

**中文对照.** 第一个调度方法是**每门名额**，借自 **TCP 窗口**。TCP 里，发送端限制**已经发出、还没确认**的数据。我们限制**已经接了要过门 6 的单、还没过门**的车，哪怕它还在别的房间、离门很远。*【指右边三条】*名额满了，派单中心就给这台车派**一单不过门的**。*【指黄色框】*名额大小按**带宽乘时延**算：故障放慢 **2.23** × 走到门口 **247 拍** × 每个方向每 500 拍 **13 台**，得到**进 15 台、出 10 台**。和 TCP 不同，**这里不丢包、名额固定**，所以有没有用必须实验。*【指左图红线】*这是实验结果：红线不管门，在途车涨到 **211 台**，每 500 拍只过 **3 台**。*【指蓝点】*蓝点用名额，在途约 **25 台**，门平均过 **26 台**。车放多了不会过得更快，**只会堵死**。

## 第 11 页　Scheduling 2: one side at a time, switch when empty

![第 11 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-11.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:15　·　配图：图 10 门口规则示意；图 11 门利用率公式**

**提示卡**

- Half-duplex + polling
- Same direction 9 ticks; switch 21 ticks
- Switch every robot: 9/21 = 43%
- Batches of 10: 90/102 = 88%
- Serve one side → drain → switch, no timer
- 4-cell zone: no network counterpart

**English.** The second method is the **door rule**, borrowed from **half-duplex links and polling**. A one-cell door is a half-duplex link: **one direction at a time**. We measured two costs. In the same direction, robots follow every **9 ticks**, but switching direction takes **21 ticks**, because the door and the other side must clear. *【指 43%】* If the door switches after every robot, it is busy only **9 over 21, or 43 percent** of the time. *【指 88%】* With batches of ten, it is **90 over 102, or 88 percent**. *【指上面三格图，从左到右】* So the door serves one side; when that side is empty, it winds down; then it switches. **No timer is needed.** *【指 4 cells】* One rule has **no network counterpart**: robots on the other side **back out of a four-cell zone** to keep the exit clear. A waiting packet never blocks a cable, but **a waiting robot blocks the way**.

**中文对照.** 第二个方法是**门口规则**，借自**半双工链路和轮询**。一格宽的门就是半双工链路：**一次一个方向**。我们测了两个代价：同方向每 **9 拍**跟过一台，换方向要 **21 拍**，因为门格和对面要先清空。*【指 43%】*每过一台就换向，门只有 **9/21 = 43%** 的时间在过车。*【指 88%】*一批放 10 台，是 **90/102 = 88%**。*【指上面三格图，从左到右】*所以门服务一边，这一边走空了就收尾，然后换向，**不需要定时器**。*【指 4 cells】*还有一条**网络里没有**：对面的车**退出 4 格的门口区**，把出口让出来。等着的数据包不会挡住网线，但**等着的车会挡路**。

## 第 12 页　A bug fix and two safety nets

![第 12 页](figures/deck/slides/slide-12.png)

**讲者 3　·　1:10　·　配图：图 12 执行器修复示意；图 13 全图上限公式**

**提示卡**

- Stuck at 22
- Credit flow control: return a freed slot at once
- Executor kept robot in old cell's queue
- 3 lines → 26
- Global cap 138 (Little's law)
- Watchdog 300 ticks; relax rule, never drop
- Hand over to Speaker 4

**English.** Third, a **bug fix**. Even with both rules, door six was **stuck at 22** robots per 500 ticks. We asked a network question: in **credit flow control**, a freed slot must be returned to the sender **at once**. *【指上排】* Checking tick by tick, we found that the official executor **kept a robot in a cell's queue after it had left**, so the next robot waited a whole planning cycle. *【指下排和 22 → 26】* **Three lines** fixed it, and the door went back to **26**. *【指右上】* Finally, **two safety nets**. Switches drop packets to recover; **we cannot drop robots, so we relax the rule instead.** Speaker 4 will show the results.

**中文对照.** 第三是**修一个程序错误**。两条规则都上了，门 6 还是**卡在每 500 拍 22 台**。我们用网络的思路去查：**信用流控**里，空位腾出来就要**立刻**还给发送端。*【指上排】*逐拍查下来，发现官方执行器**在车离开一格后，那一格的排队记录里还留着它**，后面的车白等一个规划周期。*【指下排和 22 → 26】*改了 **3 行**，门回到 **26 台**。*【指右上】*最后是**两个兜底**。按 Little 定律的**全图上限**：1.76 × 69.2 / 500 × 2.23 × 255，得到同时接单最多 **138 台**。*【指右下】*还有借自无损网络的**死锁看门狗**：车等满 **300 拍**、所在门 300 拍没有车进，就允许它这一拍不守门口规则。交换机靠丢包恢复，**我们不能丢车，所以改成放宽规则**。下面讲者 4 讲结果。
